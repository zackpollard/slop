MISSING
- The control path decides which strategy is achievable, and the simulator should gate strategies on it. A smart plug alone gives only 'discharge 16-19, then recharge immediately', worth about £0-20/yr battery-only. App TOU or schedules give fixed-window peak-cut-night, about £57-64/yr. Per-slot Agile thresholds need Home Assistant control of bypass and charge level, about £86-99/yr. App TOU modes use fixed daily windows and do not read Agile prices.
- AC input sharing: in bypass, servers plus charging share the unit's input limit (DELTA 3 Max Plus UK 10 A ≈ 2.3 kW; DELTA Pro 3 12.5 A) and the 13 A plug or smart plug (~2.99 kW). Model P_in ≤ min(acChargeW, acInputLimitW − L, 2990 − L). The model must also use a charge rate that exceeds L/η_inv + P0 whenever an online mode is modelled (the source of the -£217/-£332 artifacts).
- PV wiring realism: per-input Voc/Isc limits mean most 11-60 V units take one ordinary panel per input, so cap kWp at pvWireableTypical. Add PV capex (panels, mounting, PV cable, MC4, DC isolator, roughly £300-500 for ~0.9 kWp) and DJI's separate solar charger to payback. PV goes only into the battery and the dedicated load; anything beyond full charge is curtailed (no export).
- Transfer events and server risk: a smart-plug cut causes two transfers per cycle (grid to battery and back). Hold-up time grows a lot at partial PSU load, so the 12 ms ATX 3.1 figure is a full-load worst case. Servers with redundant PSUs can feed one PSU from the station and one from the wall, which removes outage risk but roughly halves the load shifted (PSUs load-share). Model this as a 'fraction of server load on station' input.
- Calendar ageing and SoC policy: default UPS modes hold 100% SoC 24/7 (Elite 200 V2 FAQ says so explicitly), next to warm servers. Model capacity fade (about 2-3%/yr calendar plus cycle fade to 80% at the rated cycle count) and an optional max-charge cap of 90-95%. About 400 cycles/yr means about 10 years to 4,000 cycles, but warranties are 3+2 to 5 years.
- True degradation cost per kWh: capex ÷ (cycles × usable kWh × average health) is about 13-15 p/kWh for a £1,099 / 2 kWh / 4,000-cycle unit. That is comparable to the ~18 p peak-minus-night spread, so the 2 p/kWh wear term in the DP understates it. Report payback both with and without capex-based wear.
- Bypass and standby overhead is unmeasured yet it dominates: each 10 W costs about £17.5/yr, and the AC300 states 50 W with AC on (about £88/yr). Battery-only savings of £60-100 are wiped out by 35-55 W of overhead. Recommend measuring the unit with a plug-in energy meter (bypass with no charge, at the real server load) and make P_byp and P0 user inputs.
- Inverter losses become heat. In an air-conditioned server room, add cooling energy (loss ÷ COP).
- Reserve for outage backup: if the station is also the servers' UPS, keep a reserve (e.g. 20-30% or N minutes of runtime). This reduces arbitrage capacity, and the UPS benefit itself is not monetised.
- Load isolation: Octopus consumption is whole-home import. The power station only offsets the dedicated server sub-load, so the simulator needs a separate server-load input (measured via smart plug or IPMI, or estimated from the overnight minimum) and should not apply battery dispatch to the rest of the house.
- Interaction with a plug-in solar kit: kit output already offsets the server load in bypass at midday. Charging the station from kit surplus is a 'used in conjunction with a battery' grey area under the IPS. Avoid double-counting savings in combined scenarios.
- Inter-year price variability: 2023 Agile C had a peak-minus-night spread of 18.0 p and a top6-minus-bottom6 spread of 20.8 p, against 18.4 p and 23.2 p for Oct 2025-Sep 2026. Treat results as ±10-15% and allow a different price year or tariff version.
- Several units ship grid-feeding modes (DJI Grid-Tied ESS; EcoFlow self-powered with smart meter and STREAM/PowerStream). The tool should warn that these are prohibited plug-in battery exports in GB unless installed under G98/G99, and must not count them in the plug-in power-station route.
- Vendor-cloud dependence: app TOU and the EcoFlow and Anker HA integrations rely on vendor cloud or MQTT APIs that can change. Warranty terms for continuous 24/7 UPS duty were not checked.
- Expansion £/kWh for sizing to 3 h × (L/η + P0) plus reserve: DELTA 3 extra battery £459/kWh; DELTA 3 Max Plus extra battery £390/kWh; DELTA Pro 3 extra battery £410/kWh; B300K £398/kWh; BP2000 £366/kWh; DJI about £390/kWh.
