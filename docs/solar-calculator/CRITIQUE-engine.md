### [minor] §3.2 Battery DP — SoC grid resolution
PROBLEM: A fixed socLevels of 41 gives δ = 112 Wh at 5 kWh but 45 Wh at 2 kWh. Discretisation error therefore differs by battery size, which distorts batterySizeCurve's marginal £/kWh. floor() on kUp/kDn under-uses power by up to δ per slot.
