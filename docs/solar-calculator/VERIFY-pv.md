ADDITIONAL
## Verdict

The physics in the PV-physics report checks out:
- Every formula and constant I re-checked matches pvlib 0.16.1 or GRASS r.sun.
- The PVGIS clone reproduces PVGIS to within ±0.7%.
- The PVGIS fixtures re-fetch identically.

The **data-source conclusions are wrong**:
- The "ERA5" input was actually ECMWF IFS. True ERA5 is only +2-4% on tilted planes; IFS is +9-10%.
- PVGIS's own satellite dataset (SARAH-3) is available CORS-enabled for every year through Open-Meteo's satellite API (`models=eumetsat_sarah3`). The report missed it, so the proposed -7% calibration factor is unnecessary.

The **orientation £ values** were computed on IFS and are about 9-10% high:
- With recent (2025-26) Agile prices, SSW/SW 45° edge out due south by 1-2%.
- West-vertical still loses: about £114/yr against about £162-165 for south to south-west.

The **half-hour split** helps on vertical planes. Its accuracy was overstated because the test used synthetic data; with real 1-minute data it is ±20-27% per half-hour with about +1% annual bias.
