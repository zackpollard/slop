ADDITIONAL
## Verifier summary (agile-economics)

I re-checked every hard-coded number against a fresh live pull of the Octopus API (2026-10-05 ~00:56 BST) using my own scripts, and re-ran the researcher's benchmark. **The data and statistics are all correct and reproduce to the penny.** The problems are in the policy and convention claims, plus one modelling bias.

### What is wrong or incomplete (details under corrections)
1. **Export payments.** Statutory SEG is unavailable, but Octopus gives its export tariffs to customers who buy the **Octopus plug-in bundle** (1×460 W £450 / 2×460 W £650). That includes `OUTGOING-PRIME-FIX-12M-26-06-23`, which pays 16p from 16:00-19:00 and 9p otherwise (confirmed against live API rates). The report's "default export = 0" should become an option tied to where the kit was bought.
2. **Agile Outgoing is region-specific.** In September 2026 the export multiplier k was 0.93-0.97 depending on region, and offsets differ too. The constants 0.95 / +1.30 / +7.23 hold for region C only.
3. **Levy-cut timing.** The 3.5p cut starts at **2026-03-31T22:00Z** (the start of the Agile day), not at midnight local time.
4. **Agile standing charges in the API** are one open-ended row per product version with `value_inc_vat == value_exc_vat` applied retroactively back to 2024. Historical inc-VAT values need ×1.05 before 2026-10-01. The charge also varies by version.
5. **AGILE-18-02-21** still publishes rates, with a 35p cap. Always use the user's agreement `tariff_code`.
6. **Orientation values** are about 3-5% optimistic, and the 16:00-19:00 shares depend on alignment. Two causes: the hourly PVGIS values were mapped as if centred at HH:30 (H_sun shows the stamp is HH:10), and pairing 2023 weather with 2025-26 prices loses the link between sunny days and low prices. The ranking S35 ≈ SW35 ≫ W90 still holds across all bounds.
7. Minor fixes:
   - Consumption timestamps carry a local offset, not UTC.
   - The Int8Array policy can overflow.
   - Summer midday averages ~14.1p, not 13.6p.
   - The Ofgem cap figures are at 0% VAT, not "ex VAT".
