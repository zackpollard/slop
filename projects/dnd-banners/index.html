How to print these ▾

  1. Inlay (the default) matches the reference tokens. Print the banner face down on a textured plate: the plate texture becomes the banner surface and the icon and lettering sit flush.
  2. With an AMS/MMU, download the 3MF — every part is already aligned and coloured. With a single extruder, download the STLs and insert a filament change at the detail depth (0.6 mm by default).
  3. The hanger bar prints as a wall standing up off the plate. No supports needed.
  4. Raised detail has to print face up, so pair it with the separate glue-on bracket.
  5. Measure your DM screen and set the screen thickness, then add roughly 1–1.5 mm of clearance.
