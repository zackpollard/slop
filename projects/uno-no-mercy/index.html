UNO No Mercy Scoring Rules

How Scoring Works

When a player goes out (plays all their cards) or is the last player standing, they earn points based on cards remaining in opponents' hands.

Mercy Rule Bonus

If any player reaches 25+ cards, they are knocked out. The hand winner earns 250 bonus points per player knocked out during that hand.

Winning

Keep a running tally across hands. First to 1,000 points wins the game.

Key No Mercy Rules

  • Stacking — Draw cards (+2, +4, +6, +10) can be stacked. Play a draw card of equal or higher value to pass the penalty on.
  • 7 = Swap hands — Playing a 7 lets you swap hands with another player.
  • 0 = Rotate hands — Playing a 0 forces all players to pass hands to the left.
  • Skip Everyone — Skip all other players; play again immediately.
  • Discard All — Discard all cards of the matching color from your hand.
  • Wild Color Roulette — Call a color; each opponent reveals their top card. If it matches, they draw 4.
