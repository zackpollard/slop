waze beeps

A beeps-only sound pack. One short beep for every turn — loud, obvious alerts for the things that matter.

How to use these sounds

Every sound here is synthesised live in your browser with the Web Audio API — what you hear in preview is exactly what downloads. Files are standard 16-bit mono WAVs, so they work anywhere a custom sound is accepted.

Beeps-only turn instructions in Waze

Waze lets you build a custom voice pack by supplying one clip per spoken prompt (turn left, exit, “in 500 feet”, etc.). Assign the single instruction.wav beep to every navigation prompt and Waze will only ever beep at you — drawing your attention without reading the maneuver aloud.

  1. Open Waze → Settings → Voice & sound → Waze voice.
  2. Choose Add a voice / the custom-voice recorder.
  3. For each prompt, upload (or play into the mic) instruction.wav.
  4. Use the arrival.wav chime for the “you have arrived” prompt.

Speed cameras & police alerts

Waze’s report-alert chimes (speed camera, police, crash…) aren’t replaceable from inside the app. The distinct, deliberately loud alert sounds in this pack are designed to cut through music — use them as your phone’s notification / alert tones, or in any navigation or automation app that lets you pick a custom alert sound, so each hazard is instantly recognisable.

Tip: the alert sounds use richer, brighter tones than the gentle instruction beep specifically so they stand out over a podcast or song.
