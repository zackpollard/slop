Quick Rules Reference

Goal

Be the first player to reach 200 points.

Each Round

  • Players are dealt cards one at a time. On your turn, Hit (draw another card) or Stay (bank your points).
  • If you draw a duplicate number, you bust and score 0 for the round.
  • If you collect 7 unique number cards, you get a +15 Flip 7 bonus.

Special Cards

  • Freeze — Force a player out of the round (they keep their current score).
  • Flip Three — Force a player to draw 3 cards.
  • Second Chance — Prevents one bust (discards the duplicate).

Scoring

  • Sum your number cards (0–12).
  • x2 doubles only the number card total.
  • +2, +4, +6, +8, +10 are added after the multiplier.
  • Flip 7 bonus (+15) is added last.

Formula: (Number cards × multiplier) + modifiers + Flip 7 bonus

Goal

Be the first player to reach 200 points. Same press-your-luck core as Flip 7 — but this deck is out to get you.

Each Round

  • Everyone is dealt one card face up. On your turn, Hit (draw another card) or Stay (turn your leftmost card sideways).
  • Draw a duplicate number and you bust: flip your cards face down and score 0 for the round.
  • Collect 7 different number cards for a Flip 7: the round ends immediately and you get +15.
  • Staying does not make you safe — Action and Modifier cards can still be played on you until the round ends.

Number Cards

  • 1–13 — one 1, two 2s … thirteen 13s. There is no plain 0 and no Second Chance.
  • The Zero — counts as one of your 7 cards, but your round total becomes 0 unless you Flip 7. While you hold it you must keep hitting.
  • Lucky 13 — a 13 that lets you hold a second 13 without busting. Both score 13 and both count toward Flip 7. A third 13 still busts you.
  • Unlucky 7 — when it lands in front of you, discard every other Number and Modifier card you have and keep only this 7. It can't bust you on arrival, but another 7 later will.

Modifier Cards

  • −2, −4, −6, −8, −10 and ÷2, one of each. When you draw one, keep it or give it to any player who hasn't busted — including players who have already stayed.
  • Modifiers never bust you and don't count toward Flip 7. They can be swapped, stolen, or discarded like any other card.

Action Cards

  • Resolve immediately: keep it or give it to any player who hasn't busted (even one who has stayed). If you're the only player left, you must keep it.
  • Just One More — the player draws exactly one more card, then must stay.
  • Flip Four — the player draws the next 4 cards one at a time, stopping early only on a bust or Flip 7. Action and Modifier cards drawn are resolved in order afterwards.
  • Swap — swap any two face-up cards on the table: one of yours with an opponent's, or between two other players.
  • Steal — take any face-up card from another player and add it to your line.
  • Discard — choose any player (yourself included) and one of their cards to discard.

Scoring

  • Add up your number cards (The Zero = 0, Lucky 13 = 13, Unlucky 7 = 7).
  • ÷2 halves that total, rounding down.
  • Subtract your negative modifiers. A round score can't go below 0.
  • Add the Flip 7 bonus (+15).

Formula: max(0, ⌊Number cards ÷ 2⌋ − modifiers) + Flip 7 bonus. Holding The Zero without a Flip 7 scores 0 no matter what else you have. Busted players score 0.

Brutal Mode (optional)

  • Round scores can go negative.
  • Modifier cards can be given to busted players.
  • A Flip 7 bonus can be +15 for you or −15 to another player — your choice.

Quick Rules Reference

Scoring

  • Select the number cards each player has. Sum is calculated automatically.
  • x2 doubles only the number card total.
  • +2, +4, +6, +8, +10 are added after the multiplier.
  • Toggle Flip 7 if a player collected 7 unique number cards (+15 bonus).
  • Toggle Bust if a player drew a duplicate (score = 0).

Scoring

  • Select the number cards each player ends the round with. Tap 0 for The Zero, U7 for Unlucky 7 and L13 for Lucky 13 (Lucky 13 can sit alongside a normal 13).
  • ÷2 halves the number-card total (rounded down), then −2 … −10 are subtracted. Standard scoring never drops below 0.
  • Flip 7 toggles on automatically at 7 number cards (+15). The Zero scores 0 unless its holder Flips 7.
  • Toggle Bust if a player drew a duplicate (score = 0).
  • Brutal Mode: scores can go negative, modifiers count even on a bust, and a Flip 7 can instead deal −15 to a chosen opponent.
