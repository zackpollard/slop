its question is played. Files are named opaquely (`logo-04.png`, not the brand)
so the answer is not visible in the browser's network tab.

## Cars, round two

The second car round (`cars-b/`) uses photographs from Wikimedia Commons, each under
a Creative Commons licence (`CC BY 2.0`, `CC BY 4.0`, `CC BY-SA 2.0` or `CC BY-SA 4.0`).
The photographer, the licence and the source page are recorded per image in the pack
and shown on screen while the question is played.

**These are modified copies.** Manufacturer badges, model lettering and number plates
have been cropped, blurred or painted out, because a legible badge gives the answer
away. Under the share-alike terms that applies to most of these files, the modified
images here are offered under the same licence as the original they derive from.

Per-file photographer and licence are deliberately not listed in this table, because
the credit for a car photograph often names the model and this file is served publicly
alongside the quiz. The app shows each credit at the right moment instead.
