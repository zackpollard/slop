📖 Quick Rules Reference

Roles & Win Conditions

  • Sheriff — Eliminate all Outlaws and the Renegade. The Sheriff is the only revealed role.
  • Deputy — Protect the Sheriff. Deputies win when the Sheriff wins.
  • Outlaw — Kill the Sheriff. Outlaws win immediately if the Sheriff is eliminated.
  • Renegade — Be the last one standing. Must kill everyone, including the Sheriff (but last).

Turn Structure

  1. Draw 2 cards from the deck (unless character ability says otherwise).
  2. Play any number of cards — but only one BANG! per turn (unless you're Willy the Kid or have a Volcanic).
  3. Discard down to your current life points (hand limit = HP).

Distance

Players sit in a circle. Distance = minimum number of seats between two players (counting either direction around the circle), skipping eliminated players. Equipment modifies distance:

  • Mustang / Hideout — Other players see you at distance +1
  • Scope — You see other players at distance -1
  • Weapons — Determine your BANG! range (default Colt .45 = range 1)

Key Cards

  • BANG! — Shoot a player within weapon range. They must play a Missed! or lose 1 HP.
  • Missed! — Cancel a BANG! aimed at you.
  • Beer — Regain 1 life point (not usable with only 2 players left).
  • Saloon — All players regain 1 life point.
  • Stagecoach / Wells Fargo — Draw 2 / 3 cards.
  • Panic! — Draw a card from a player at distance 1.
  • Cat Balou — Force any player to discard a card.
  • Duel — Challenge any player. Take turns discarding BANG! cards; first who can't loses 1 HP.
  • Indians! — All other players discard a BANG! or lose 1 HP.
  • Gatling — All other players lose 1 HP (counts as a BANG! to all).
  • Barrel — "Draw!" when targeted by BANG!; heart = Missed!
  • Dynamite — Passes each turn; "Draw!" 2-9 of spades = 3 damage!
  • Jail — Target "Draws!" next turn; heart = escape, otherwise skip turn. Cannot jail the Sheriff.

Elimination Rewards

  • Killing an Outlaw — The killer draws 3 cards.
  • Sheriff kills a Deputy — The Sheriff discards their entire hand and all cards in play.
